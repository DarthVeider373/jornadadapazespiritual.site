Our Story

A family passion born in the rolling hills of North Yorkshire

🏔️

From Our Farm to Your Table

Nestled in the picturesque village of Thornton Rust, near Leyburn in the Yorkshire Dales, Wensleydale Ice Cream is a family-run business dedicated to producing the finest artisan ice cream.

Since 2012, we've been crafting small-batch ice cream using traditional methods and the freshest ingredients sourced from local farms across Wensleydale. Our commitment to quality means every tub is made with care, passion, and a deep love for our beautiful dale.
